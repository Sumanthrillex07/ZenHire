## Design System Rules

The repository root contains DESIGN.md.

Treat DESIGN.md as the source of truth for all visual
design decisions.

For every new UI component or modification:

- Follow its color palette.
- Follow its typography.
- Follow its spacing and radius conventions.
- Follow its component styling principles.
- Maintain consistency across all routes.
- Support both light and dark themes.

Reuse existing shared components.

Do not introduce arbitrary colors, typography,
shadows or styling conventions.

Preserve all existing application functionality.
